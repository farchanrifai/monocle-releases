# Coffee Journal 0.2.0 (build 5)

Released 2 October 2026. Personal café stamps and a more useful Want to try list for iOS 17 and later.

## New

- Personalize each café branch stamp with a rounded, oval, or postage frame, sage/terracotta/espresso/blue ink, and a simple icon. Automatic designs remain available, and the editor shows a live preview.
- Give your Home stamp a personal name and design, without adding an address.
- Save a café note for an order to try, a recommendation, or a detail you want to remember. Notes appear in Want to try and the café's history.
- A newly confirmed at-café coffee completes that café's Want to try entry. Delivery and unconfirmed coffees keep it on your list. Undo restores an automatically completed wish when no later confirmed visit remains; manual wishlist changes take precedence.

## Improved

- Native, full-row stamp menus with clear selection values and accessible tap targets. Passport uses a single stamp column at accessibility text sizes.
- Adding a matching saved café reuses its identity and keeps existing notes/designs when optional fields are left blank or automatic. Entered personalization is saved.
- Backups now include stamp designs, café notes, Home settings, and wishlist completion state. Café and delivery memories without a saved place can also be backed up.
- Explicit migration from the current app's earlier journal format, with existing memories, prices, usuals, favorites, and photo references retained in simulator verification. Earlier backups from this app remain readable.

## Validation

76 distinct tests passed on the iOS 26.5 iPhone 17 Pro simulator: 61 data, migration, backup, photo, and rendering tests plus 15 user-flow tests. The four new Passport flows passed a focused rerun after final accessibility and test corrections. Compatibility tests use a populated database and photo backup generated by the shipped v0.1.3 code, then migrate, reopen, and restore them. Backup tests also cover repeated restoration and two visits saved within the same second.

Light and dark previews were reviewed, and the café and Home personalization flows also passed in dark appearance. The Home editor and single-column Passport also passed with larger accessibility text. The unsigned arm64 device Release build succeeded. IPA archive integrity, bundle identity, version 0.2.0 (build 5), and iOS 17 minimum OS were verified.

## Installation

This IPA is unsigned and keeps the existing `com.farchan.CoffeeJournal` app identity. Keep a journal backup before updating. SideStore/LiveContainer installation, data-preserving updates, camera, photo cutout quality, and Files/share sheets still require physical-device verification.

The older Coffee Journal Prototype remains a separate app. Its journal and backup format do not transfer to this app.
