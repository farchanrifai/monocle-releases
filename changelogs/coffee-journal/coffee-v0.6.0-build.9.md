# Coffee Journal 0.6.0 (build 9)

Released 3 October 2026. A richer monthly reflection for your coffee memory book, for iOS 17 and later.

## New

- Open Monthly reflection from the recap below your Journal collection. Browse months without leaving the reflection, with illustrated cups and the same warm paper design.
- Revisit your most logged drinks, new café stamps, coffees marked “Would order again,” and moments with a note or an occasional photo. Tap a highlight to open the recorded coffee or café. Longer sections offer See all, including every drink tied for the highest count.
- Café discoveries reflect the first physical visit recorded for each saved branch across your journal. Delivery and unresolved places do not count; edits, backdated coffees, deletions, and café merges update the highlights.
- See coffees logged, home coffees, cafés visited, and recorded spending with its price coverage. Missing prices remain unknown. Frequency and “Would order again” remain separate.
- Write an optional personal note for each month, up to 2,000 characters. Save, edit, cancel a draft, or clear and save to remove it. Empty months can still hold a note and remain reachable from the Journal month picker.

## Privacy and backups

Monthly notes stay on-device and travel in native journal backups. Shared scrapbooks and coffee-entry CSV exports leave monthly notes out. Restoring a backup keeps an existing note for the same month, including one created separately. The temporary sample journal allows reading reflections but does not save personal writing.

Schema and backup format advance to version 5. The explicit migration preserves the released coffee, cup, café, logo, usual-order, and Home-preference fields. Native backups from versions 1–4 remain readable. The café logo catalog and selected artwork format are unchanged.

## Validation

145 distinct native tests passed on the iOS 26.5 iPhone 17 Pro simulator: all 134 data, migration, backup, image, and rendering tests; six reflection flows; and five affected logging, journal/share, Passport, and logo flows. The new coverage includes drink ties, local-month boundaries, first physical visits and backdated records, note persistence and transaction rollback, malformed backups, duplicate restore, and privacy exclusions. All four See all collections were exercised with a longer month, including opening older highlights.

Compatibility tests migrated and reopened populated databases created by shipped versions of the current app, including v0.5.0. The v4 fixture was generated by the unmodified released code; a complete archive comparison verifies coffees, usuals, cup colors, café logos and personalization, Home settings, photos, and precise timestamps before saving and reopening a new monthly note. Native v1–v4 backups remain readable.

Reflection highlights and note editing also passed in dark mode and with accessibility-large text, two flows in each. Light, dark, and larger-text screenshots were reviewed. The unsigned arm64 Release IPA was validated for ZIP integrity, unsigned status, bundle identity, version 0.6.0 (build 9), and iOS 17 minimum OS. Packaged café catalog artwork matches the source; synthetic UI fixtures are excluded from the Release binary. The publication helper's 13 tests passed.

## Installation

This IPA is unsigned and keeps the existing `com.farchan.CoffeeJournal` app identity. Keep a journal backup before updating. SideStore/LiveContainer installation, data-preserving updates, camera, photo cutout quality, and Photos/Files/share sheets still require physical-device verification.

The older Coffee Journal Prototype remains a separate app. Its journal and backup format do not transfer to this app.
